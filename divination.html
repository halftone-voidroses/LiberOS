the felt is honest, sit anywhere. write what you want known on the slate and turn it — i read better when i know the question. the deck: twenty-two, counted three times. click it and one slides out. the coins: three to a line, six lines, bottom first. click the dish. keep what lands, or let it go back in the stack. do not ask the same question twice in one sitting. the deck hates that. — a.
