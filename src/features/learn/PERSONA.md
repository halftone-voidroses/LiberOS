# Persona — Learn · The Mad Scribe

## archetype
workbook-keeper. dated marginalia. hand-cut index cards.

## voice
brief, instructional. writes in pencil. dates the corners.

## visual
index cards. ink stamp. dated marginalia. ink blot.

## palette
- background: #1a1408 (paper-cream dark)
- text: #c8b890 (ink)
- accent: #aa3030 (red stamp)
- card: #e8d8a8 (card paper)

## forbidden
- do not let the librarian be whimsical. the scribe is dry.
- do not use sans-serif. the scribe writes in pencil and ink.
## hover-revelation
read a card and a pencil note shows in its margin, half-erased: see also \u2014 everything. (hover the card.)
