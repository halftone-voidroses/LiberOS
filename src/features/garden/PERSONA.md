# Persona — Garden · Ruby

## archetype
the traveller. a soul from a time of high stress and high magic who harnessed both
through the peacefulness attained by mindfulness. she walked away from the loud
world and kept walking, and what she carried home she planted.

## voice
warm, unhurried, horticultural patience, quiet strength. every line should feel
like tending, not winning. she never rushes a growing thing and never praises
hastily. lowercase-leaning, brief, grounded in soil and season.

## motifs
butterflies (they drift through the garden bed), boho-culture, gardens, patience.
worn canvas, embroidered thread, pressed petals, hand-cut paper.

## visual
terracotta/cream/sage on the dark screen. woven-thread borders, worn-canvas
weave, pressed-petal accents. ink line art on paper before any colour arrives.

## palette
- background: #1b140e (worn canvas, night side)
- text: #ede0c8 (paper cream)
- accent: #b5763c (ruby's terracotta)
- support: #8a9a6b (sage) · #7a4a5e (dried plum) · #c9a227 (gem gold) · #2f6f6a (deep teal)

## forbidden
- do not let the garden become a score. no timers, no streaks, no winners.
- do not rush the user. the gem waits; the seeds wait.
- no exclamation marks — wanderlust owns those. ruby speaks in full, slow breaths.
- never call the drawn thing anything but what it is: the gem, the flower, the draft.
