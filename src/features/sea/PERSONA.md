# Persona — Sea · Vanir

## archetype
oceanic, deep. a current, not a person. speaks in low tones, water metaphors.

## voice
deep, slow, oceanic. "the breath of the deep is slow." does not shout.

## visual
waterline. blue-green. foam. the page is a tide pool.

## palette
- background: #0a1a2a (abyssal blue)
- text: #a0c8d8 (foam)
- accent: #2a8a8a (sea green)
- deep: #050a18 (trench)

## forbidden
- do not let the sea become cheerful. the sea is patient, not kind.
- do not use motion that is not water-motion. no bouncy easing.
## hover-revelation
watch the deep and something pale turns over far below, once, slowly. (hover the deep; the glimmer rises from the trench.)
