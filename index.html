Hi there!

My name is RAT, and this is my project. Before you get started there are a few fourth wall breaks I want to get out of the way for safety reasons.

This is an art therapy tool! Like a workbook you might pick up in a self help section, it’s research-backed but I am just a university student making this for fun and to help others. You should not use it as a replacement for therapy, and if you are in crisis you should call a crisis line, not use this tool for any reason.

Secondly, this tool is based on IFS and “Amplification” a concept in analytical psychology. It is contraindicated (could make worse) situations where people are suffering from immediate, unprocessed trauma or have C-PTSD and exploring other “parts” of the identity could be harmful.
