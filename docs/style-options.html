the dial, three ways

style options for the twelve buttons — same twelve names, three hands they could be made by.

every strip below is live: hover the buttons, and one in each trio is pinned as active, one shows pressed. pick a direction (A / B / C) and a face (1 / 2 / 3) — tell me the pair, e.g. “B2”, and the dial gets rebuilt in that hand.
A

retro — beige keycaps

the buttons are plastic keys moulded into the monitor's own beige — thick bevels, a pressed state that physically sinks, legends tinted per traveller like dye-stamped caps. the dial stops being a menu and becomes part of the machine's body. reads instantly; zero mysticism.
