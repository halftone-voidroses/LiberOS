the aesthetic sweep

a critical pass over the machine — what could be depicted more intricately, and the wheel, redrawn.

part one audits the surfaces: what exists, what more intricate would look like, and what it costs — grouped free wins, worth a session, one day. part two puts the shipped dial beside a dial where each traveller designed their own. two ideas were cut for lying (a mood-ring bezel, encouragement copy) — the report says where.
1

things that could be depicted more intricately

free wins — pure css/svg, an afternoon

the led is a pilot flame, not a dot

now — a static red circle pulsing on a fixed loop.

a two-phase candle-flicker (fast jitter inside a slow breathe), a tiny wax mound building under it that grows with visits, and a warm 1px halo reflecting on the plastic below.

one keyframe set + a radial-gradient pseudo keyed to the existing patina classes. reduced-motion: static flame.

the boot title typesets itself

now — "LIBER.OS" fades in as one block.

letterpress — each glyph lands individually with a 1px press-sink and ink-spread, sequenced like a hand-set print. ENTER follows with a physical thunk.

per-letter spans + staggered delays; the boot already has the sound engine for the thunk.

the status line is ticker-tape

now — a bevelled plate; text swaps every 12s with a fade.

perforated tape edges (punch-holes along the plate), and each phrase arrives in small horizontal steps like a teletype advancing — not a fade.

dashed-border pseudo-elements + steps() on a translate. the plate is the status line's own; nothing shared reaches the apps.

the carvings collect verdigris

now — patina deepens the carvings in sepia and brightness tiers.

the two copper-bearing carvings (the stone's inlay, relation's ring) grow a green oxide bloom in the recesses as visits accumulate — verdigris is what copper does, and it is the one color the bezel never uses.

a green blend layer on the inlay pseudo-elements, keyed to patina-2/3. the room's own chemistry.

the glass has a glare that wanders

now — static curvature shading and vignette.

a slow diagonal glare band crossing the tube every ~40s — barely visible, 8s crossing, warm-white at 3%. the tube is glass; glass reflects the room.

one rotated gradient pseudo on .screen with a long translate loop. reduced-motion gated.

worth a session — an hour of craft each

the casting stone gathers dust while you draw

now — the sigil revelation shows dust in the cracks on hover.

while the hand is actually down, canvas particles settle into the stroke grooves and stay — the drawing physically accumulates the room.

the dust canvas already exists; a settle-mode keyed to pointerdown on the sigil canvas. the particles are the room's, not new ones.

the wax room's light follows the conversation

now — three fixed candle flames above the chat transcript.

the light pool on the tablet shifts toward whichever turn is newest — her words lean the glow cool, yours lean it warm. the newest message is always the best-lit.

a gradient overlay whose origin interpolates to the last turn's side; one transition, no new loops.

the sea is deep, not flat

now — a depth gauge, breath rings, one pale hover-shape below.

two parallax silhouette layers — far kelp and a nearer shelf — drifting at different rates with the mouse depth, so descending feels like passing through water, not sliding a slider.

two silhouette layers translating at ratios of the existing depth value. no new input.

the ledger cracks a seal before it unbinds

now — release removes the row instantly, with the thunk.

on release, the row's wax seal cracks (a split running through it over 300ms) and then the row lifts away — destruction you can see, honest about what just happened.

a two-half seal glyph + a keyframed exit transform. the relation app's own css only.

the loading ritual has drive chatter

now — "Now Loading", the fate line, and a wipe.

a low synthesized drive-chatter under the text — seek, track, seek, read — so the machine sounds like it is reading something real. fades with the wipe.

filtered noise bursts in sound.js's existing voice architecture; a 'chatter' kind wired to the loading page only.

one day — needs new machinery

the room keeps real hours

now — the radial clock shows the time; the room is the same darkness at 3pm and 3am.

the ambient glow drifts with the actual local hour — deep red at night, a colder near-black at noon. never announced, never a "theme": just a room with windows it does not have. uses the clock that already ticks.

an hour-derived class (room-noon/dusk/night) set in shadow.js; three gradient overrides in room.css. it is light, not motion — reduced-motion does not hold it back.

the cohort has a waveform in the static

now — "the cohort becomes louder" is overlay resolution tiers and status-line voice.

at the loudest tier, the static canvas occasionally carries a faint coherent waveform — a pulse in the noise, one second, gone. the cohort is not text; it is a shape in the signal.

a canvas draw keyed to tier-3 + the seeded ambient scheduler that already exists. one session of canvas work.

cut on purpose — two ideas that lied

the bezel mood-ring — a bezel tint that shifts with app or state. cut: the corrupted default is a truth about the machine, not an indicator to restyle.

encouragement copy — reassurance after saves or releases. cut: flattery is contempt even from a CRT. the machine does not praise; the covenant already legislated this and the sweep respects it.

the sweep proposes intricacy, not decoration — and never a second voice in the room.

what the unified grammar gains

instant scanning — twelve tiles read as one instrument. cheaper to keep coherent as the machine grows. the phosphor family already matches entity404's room and the CRT premise itself.

what the bespoke hand gains

the dial becomes the graveyard's lesson applied to navigation — every tile is a room you have stood in, made by someone who was there. warmer, stranger, and the only place all twelve hands sit side by side.

the hybrid is real

bespoke tile backs (their material, their mark) with the unified phosphor label kept above — scanning stays, presence arrives. the material recipes already exist in each app's shipped styles; the dial would be borrowing, not inventing.

this is the recommendation if the bespoke hand wins.

everything here keeps the frieze, the motes, the crossing ritual, and the reduced-motion gates exactly as shipped. nothing proposed nags, scores, or flatters.
