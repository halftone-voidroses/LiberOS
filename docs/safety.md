# Safety & ethics frame

> Liber.OS does shadow work, invitation-only darkness, and evocative
> challenge. The covenant says "agency over protection" — this file defines
> how that holds clinically, not despite it.

## Principles

1. **Agency with scaffolding.** Users choose intensity; the OS never hides
   the exit and never blocks content behind interrogation. Safety features
   are opt-in and visible, not gates.
2. **Grounded in research-backed practice.** Exercises map to DBT skills
   (distress tolerance, emotion regulation), art-therapy externalization,
   and structured reflective writing. `data/exercises.json` carries the
   author's research notes per exercise.
3. **The shadow overdrive is earned, explicit, and reversible.** The `extc`
   rite is deliberately hidden but must always have a visible way back
   (the corrupted default is home). Reversed fortunes and cruel prompts
   must never target crisis content — see intensity gating below.

## Intensity gating (planned)

- The prompt engine tags templates with an intensity band (`low | mid |
  deep`). `deep` templates (directly challenging a protection, naming the
  feared self) are excluded until the Cohort has an established relation
  history (e.g. ≥ 5 relations, ≥ 3 visits) — graduated exposure, not a wall.
- The Sea release accepts intensity 1–5; intensity ≥ 4 states (once, calmly)
  that the deep receives it either way — no performance demanded.

## Crisis surface (planned)

- A permanent, quiet affordance (status line keyword + a listing in Learn)
  with region-agnostic crisis lines (e.g. 988 in the US, local equivalents)
  and grounding exercises. Never an interrupt modal; always one gesture
  away.
- The owner is separately prototyping crisis-classification logic (see the
  author's related work); integration is a future decision requiring its
  own design pass and false-positive analysis.

## Research ethics (grad-theory layer)

- If session logs are ever collected (see `docs/gamification.md`
  measurement section): opt-in, local-first, exportable, deletable, never
  transmitted by the app itself.
- Any claim presented by the citations traveller must carry its source
  (`data/citations.json`); the OS distinguishes "the machine's mythology"
  (Wanderlust) from "what research says" (Raison/Archivist voice).
- Content derived from the author's own theories is marked as such in
  Learn/Method — the colonial-bridging thesis is presented as thesis, not
  established finding.
