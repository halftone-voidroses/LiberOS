the twelve marks

icon suites for the dial — every mark is the favicon its traveller cut for their own app, redrawn until the twelve read as one family.

each icon below is the little sign a traveller hung over their own door — then changed into something with parity across the options: same grid, same weight, same frame, per suite. above every mark sits its phosphor tile — a small glass label, vt323, dim, naming the app. every strip is live: hover the tiles; one is pinned active, one pressed. pick a suite (s1–s5). it combines with the button material (a / b / c) from the earlier catalog — two separate decisions, e.g. “s2 with material b”.
s1

carvings — engraved in the bezel

the first grammar. every traveller's own favicon re-cut by mistress physius' chisel: one stroke weight (1.6), one 24-unit grid, one corner radius, stone-dark tiles. the strokes are cream tinted faintly toward each traveller's accent — close enough to read as one carving, warm enough to tell apart. the mark is the groove; nothing is filled that the chisel did not bite.

s2

phosphor bitmaps — entity404's grid

entity404 took the twelve and rasterized them onto a 12×12 cell grid — the bbs icon grammar, chunky on purpose. every glyph is solid pixel runs, no anti-aliasing, each burning in its traveller's phosphor with a glass bloom. the cursor blinks because it should. reads at any size a tube can render.

s3

wax seals — pressed by e-lizabeth

e-lizabeth's wax grammar pressed across the whole set: one seal diameter, one rim, one bone-colored impression per mark. deep red wax for most; near-black for the two she keeps for herself (chat, relation). the motif is not drawn — it is the dent the stamp leaves.

schematics — raison drafts the set

s5

rubbing — pete rubs the carvings

ravaging pete taped paper over the bezel carvings and rubbed all twelve in charcoal: chalk-white line on black, edges caught rough by the paper's tooth (one displacement filter shared by every mark), one wobbly rubbing frame shared by the set. slightly irregular on purpose — the unevenness is the same across all twelve, which is what makes it a family.
