# Inspiration

> Narrative source for the LiberVacui1.0 user experience. Read this before writing any feature — it defines the *feeling* of the OS, not just the structure.

---

Imagine your mind is a village, the people in it populate your dreams, and amongst them exist depictions of all of your archetypes. In this world there are many places, but the place housing the Liber Vacui is special.

(You enter a dark room, dust drifts in the background like stars in the void. The room is empty save for a well worn table and chair, and a mysterious computer. As you approach you see the screen flick on, a brilliant terrifying crimson and large text fills the centre

> **Thank you for choosing**
> **Liber.OS**
>
> [start]

As you sit down, the start sign beckons to you like a siren in the old greek seas, there is a coolness emanating from the computer, and an unsettling heat emanating from the monitor. You click start.

> **Liber Vacui**
> **Now Loading**
> ==fate appreciates your patronage==

You wait in nervous anticipation as the program loads, faint echoes of souls long past move slowly in the background.

(At last. The desktop loads. On the desktop there is a single black ?, it's large, inviting, and eclipsed with flickering pink/yellow flames.

Clicking the question mark opens a window in the middle of the screen..)

> Wanderlust is being summoned, please wait…
>
> I summon you from somewhere else..
> …
> …To find the pieces of ourselves.. *(screen shakes)*
> ….With violet eyes and sun like skin… *(chat window appears)*
> …Come to the void and sing again..
> …

(You see a thick pink circle with a yellow circle inside, and a single blinking white eye in the very centre, underneath is a chat window. The circle begins to speak.)

> (There are next options after each chat message to proceed or go back)
>
> Oh! Hello!
> I am here from the 'pataphysical realm to assist you on your journey.
>
> I have been called many things over the years..
>
> Fate..
> Chance..
> Destiny..
> The Wheel..
> Samsara..
> *(As she names these her colour briefly flickers and changes between forms)*
>
> But you may call me Wanderlust, for that is the drive behind my whims, both good and evil.
>
> Your travels have been long, and difficult, but rest easy traveller.
>
> You have already given yourself to the liber vacui. Many have came before you, and have made this journey easier.
>
> The first was Mistress Physius. She practiced the ancient hermetic traditions and was a pious women. Her soul lives in the Novus as the stonecutter, invoke her. Draw your sigil.

*(Chat flickers, Raison takes over the visual language)*

> **Raison**: Oh good. The chat is open. She hates this kind of thing but good UI design is important for the user experience. This will appear on the desktop, from there, explore the apps and features made by other travellers like yourself. Some of them are self-help and reflective tools, others divination, some allow you to save artifacts to your desktop.

> **Wanderlust**: INSOLENT WORM *(Screen shakes, Wanderlust reappears)*

> **Raison**: Wait don't send me b-

> **Wanderlust**: The sigil represents your shadow and an entity lives within this computer that will speak to you through software, signals, and signs. Listen to what it says, what is the other voice telling you? How does it want to be seen?

> **Raison**: Ugh. Once you add your first artifact to your desktop you can declare a relation by clicking on the artifact, this links it to the sigil.
>
>  As a practical example, say your sigil represents your unconscious, unintegrated fear of inadequacy and you draw a card from Arcana's tarot deck. She reveals to you the tower (destruction breeds creation). This reminds you of how, despite feeling inadequate, you are particularly good at handling rejection (rare, but if this is you, congrats!). You may say that the tower -> protects -> the sigil. You establish the relation.
>
> Once you've done that, it will orbit the sigil, and over time the associations with it will grow.
>
> Your eventual goal is to establish "communication" with your cohort, illustrate them using the research backed prompts and ideas laid-out (coming soon)

*(Screen shakes, chat returns)*

> **Wanderlust**: Enough of that. The more you communicate with your cohort, the louder they will become, so says the travellers anyways. Remember to always love and care for your cohort, they are you.

*(Screen fades away, text re-appears across the full screen, undeniably wanderlust, gothic thick spooky text that appears above even the monitor)*

> By the end, you should understand:   I arise the same but different.

*(The text fades away and the desktop becomes illuminated. This is the first time you really took in the aesthetics of it, what with all the.. magic.. the question mark is still there, but not glowing. Instead there is large, blocky text on the bottom, it's a semicircle that takes up the bottom third of the screen, large in the middle it says "sigil" and there are two reactive arrows on each side of it, clicking them cycles between different menu options, the non-selected options greying out and moving to the sides. This whole thing is very.. retro.. you think to yourself. You take a deep breath and click the button labeled "Sigil")*

> Sigil Loading..

*(You notice a glyph light up on the thick monitor bevel. Once it lights up the software opens, the desktop dims and a glow surrounds the die-cut stone tablet that is sitting in the middle of the screen. Cracks surround the tablet and there is text)*

> Sigil Casting:
> Step One: Write down your intention
> [ put the intention here ]
> Step Two: Let your intuition guide you, draw the intention.
> There is no step three.

*(Below the text there are words etched in the stone — air, water, fire, earth, spirit — each one changes the chisel-cursor to a different color. You take a deep breath before drawing on the canvas inlayed into the stone. The colours are what you would expect, except for spirit which is glowing. You put all of your might into this sigil, eventually clicking the "SAVE" text etched into the stone. The screen disappears and the sigil application becomes scratched out, you think to make another sigil you would have to reset your browser content.)*

*(You flick through the other options, each app more unique and interesting than the last until you reach one simply labeled "Shadow". As you hover over it the screen bubbles, becomes dark, and thoughts you dare not repeat to yourself move in the air behind the monitor in the dusty room. You understand this is your shadow speaking to you. You click the label. A password window pops up, thankfully you do not have the password because if you did, the entire operating system would become dark, twisted, and mean. Fortunes read would be reversed, cohort chats would be evocative and cruel to your insecurities, the only thing that would stay the same is Whimsy Wow's therapy tools.)*

## Cast (locked by this narrative)

| Entity | Form | Voice | Domain |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wanderlust** | Thick pink circle, yellow inner circle, single blinking white eye center. Color flickers between forms when naming her past identities (Fate/Chance/Destiny/Wheel/Samsara) | Gothic thick spooky, all-caps, dramatic. Screen shakes when angry | The 'pataphysical realm. Driver of whims both good and evil |
| **Raison** | Takes over the visual language (line / wireframe). Slips into chat uninvited | Dry, snarky, UX-obsessed. Insists on good design | Practical guide. Explains mechanics (relation, orbit, cohort) |
| **Mistress Physius** | Stonecutter, lives in the Novus | Pious, hermetic | First traveller, established the sigil tradition |

## Mechanical Truths (locked by this narrative)

1. **The sigil is the shadow.** It is unique. After you save it, the Sigil app becomes scratched out — you cannot make another without resetting browser content.
2. **Relation** is declared by clicking an artifact on the desktop, which links it to the sigil with a verb (e.g. `tower -> protects -> sigil`).
3. **Artifacts orbit the sigil** over time as associations grow.
4. **Cohort is the eventual goal**: a network of illustrated entities established via the apps (research-backed prompts, coming soon).
5. **Shadow is the overdrive**: a separate `extc` password, reveals the dark/twisted version of the OS. Tarot reverses, cohort goes cruel, only Whimsy Wow stays wholesome.

## Visual Beats (locked by this narrative)

- The question mark is **flaming** (pink/yellow) when it summons Wanderlust; it is **plain and non-glowing** once the tutorial ends and the desktop is revealed.
- The "I arise the same but different" text appears as gothic thick spooky text **above the monitor**, breaking the screen boundary.
- The dial at the bottom is a **semicircle** that takes up the bottom third of the screen, with the selected label large in the middle and non-selected options greying and shifting to the sides.
- The Sigil app shows a **die-cut stone tablet** with cracks, glow, chisel cursor with 5 element colors (air/water/fire/earth/spirit, spirit glows).
- The Shadow icon makes the **screen bubble and darken** on hover, and "thoughts you dare not repeat" appear in the air behind the monitor in the dusty room.
