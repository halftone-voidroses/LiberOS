The hand finds a real object

The slope turns toward the visitor. The 3 × 4 caps are arranged as a compact block, not a long strip. Each is a smaller square cap with a shallow sloped top, a lamp set into that top face, and a dark enamel room label on the upper front face. The rows overlap slightly like a real compact keyboard viewed from the desk, so the caps read as one instrument instead of twelve floating cards. The home key has a darker brass cap and a maker's mark; labels remain legible as the cap travels. The selected key sits low. A visited key keeps a spent ember. The last socket is a struck blanking plug, not an absent button.

There is no sound requirement. The click may be a detent, but the lowered cap and lit lamp carry the whole state when sound is off, focus is visible, or motion has been reduced.

Give the glass back to the night

The constellation is the desktop's central object. The margin sheet is paper at the edge and a working scratch pad: a visitor can revise the line, then move it to Journal. The home key is on the case, so the sky gets to stay quiet: no task register, no productivity weather, no row of widgets asking to be sorted.

Objects, not widgets

  • the stone: fixed star, the one thing every room has helped make more particular.
  • kept things: releases, developed pages, tickets, and other artifacts become points of light on a stable orbit.
  • the notepad: a folded corner, pencil pressure, one place to scratch and attach a thought without opening a second app.
  • the background: a shelf, candle, waterline, or red string can gather behind the graph when the room has actually earned it.

The wall refuses

  • tasks, streaks, progress bars, and “today” lists;
  • automatic node shuffling after every keep;
  • notification badges competing with the sky;
  • modals that cover the field for ordinary reading.

ROOM HOOKThe astronomy state must leave a named material behind the CRT: a new star on the shelf or a line in the board. The glass is the index; the room remains the memory.

04

Build register

The first pass should make the body honest before it makes the wallpaper elaborate.

pass one / case

  1. Remove the radial strip, popup index, and long-press dependency from the desktop.
  2. Mount the bottom keybank in the existing monitor composition; reserve the right-hand Liberchat gutter.
  3. Map the existing room destinations and state.visited into a stable physical order.
  4. Implement pressed, visited, locked, focus, sound-off, and reduced-motion states.

pass two / glass

  1. Expand the constellation across the recovered screen area with stable anchor positions.
  2. Promote scratch notes to the diegetic paper surface: open, edit, attach, dismiss, save.
  3. Connect artifacts and relation edges to the existing room hooks; do not duplicate room furniture on the desktop.
  4. Test at 1280×800 and 439px with the sidecar, labels, exit, and focus still honest.
THE DECISIONBuild the left-aligned 3 × 4 keybank as the navigation floor. Give HOME a distinct dark brass cap, keep the eleven room doors in a stable three-row order, and let the twelfth socket remain a struck blanking plug until it earns a name. The square tube and short console keep the sidecar visually separate; the small keybank floats just above the lower bezel as a serviceable object rather than becoming a second screen. Let the machine notice visits through lamps, let the room behind the CRT hold the lasting evidence, and let the margin sheet remain a piece of paper that can be sent to Journal.
