00Verdict

LiberOS reads as a ledger of promises kept. The stated laws — grounding, determinism, one voice, no randomness in anything the user can perceive — are honored exactly where they cost the most. The drift lives at the seams: five artifact registries that quietly disagree, a canonical reading face that never ships, selector forks in the largest stylesheet, and a spec that lost two of its own apps on the way to the build.

None of what follows breaks the machine. Smoke passes, the verification suite is unusually good, and the determinism discipline holds under test. What follows is the gap between the covenant the project writes for itself and the code that ships — each finding with its evidence, a confidence rating, and the strongest reading against calling it a defect.

02What is right

A critique that only counts wounds misses the discipline. These are the rules the code actually keeps.

  • Grounding is law — enforced data/twelve-works.data.js:5-7 — citations and exercises can only be claimed by the works that provably carry them; trash carries no exercise and no citation, and the verification suite sweeps every raison panel for dev-spec text (scripts/verify-fixes.mjs M1, 14 pages).
  • Determinism where outcomes matter Prompt composition is seeded per (relation, salt) and returns identical text (scripts/verify-prompt-engine.mjs a/d), ambient prompts are planned deterministically per visit, capped at two, gated on relations, and never repeat (scripts/verify-gamification.mjs). Booth bests are strict high-water marks with junk rejected.
  • An unusually honest test suite 25-step end-to-end smoke walking the entire tutorial with real assertions — including the regression pin that the BIG ? must not auto-open chat (smoke.mjs:60-65) and the D0001 dial rename (smoke.mjs:158-161). Gamification tests use 100-day-old timestamps on purpose to prove presence, not streaks, still counts.
  • The dist is the source, byte for byte scripts/app-dist.cjs copies root pages + src/styles/data/fonts and verifies every file with sha256 before exit; presentation/ is deliberately excluded and documented why. The Windows workflow rewrites the Tauri targets to nsis/msi.
  • inert, not aria-hidden, for live surfaces Closed interactive containers use the inert attribute (scripts/verify-fixes.mjs M4) — focus is truly removed, not just hidden from the tree, which survives the file://-probe build.
