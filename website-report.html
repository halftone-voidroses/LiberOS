A haunted operating system
that turns attention into artifacts.

LiberOS is not a collection of utilities. It is a local-first ritual machine: one CRT, many rooms, a buddy-stone at the centre, and a book that remembers what the visitor chose to keep. This report is the product pitch, the room-by-room deck, and the next-pass brief.

01Product thesis

The visitor does not consume content; they make a thing, decide whether it matters, and give it a place in a living system.

promiseMake inner material handleable

Stone, dream, game, sea-text, chat transcript, seed, card, and note become concrete artifacts. The interface gives feelings a surface without pretending to diagnose, cure, or know the visitor better than they do.

positionTherapy-adjacent, not therapy-replacing

The boot warning and Hard Nights page establish the boundary. The voice is strange and literary, but the product contract is local, explicit, and safety-conscious.

moatRelational memory

The desktop is a constellation rather than a dashboard. Keeping, binding, releasing, and revisiting create a personal visual grammar that generic journaling apps do not have.

constraintDeliberate opacity

The machine whispers. That is the brand, but it raises the cost of discovery. The next step should add signposts without flattening the haunted atmosphere into onboarding chrome.

04First-use journey

The product should feel mysterious after it is understood, not before.

What is working now

The consent gate establishes the boundary. The tutorial demonstrates the fake chain without writing false artifacts. The desktop invites the first real cast. The smoke flow proves a real stone, Tarot, Games wheel, constellation binding, migration, slots, notes, and clean reset.

Where the visitor still hesitates

The dial shows only three rooms at a time. Several attractions are pointer-first. Overlay semantics are duplicated. Tarot/I Ching and room-specific saves do not yet feel like one artifact vocabulary. These are the next product costs, not reasons to flatten the world.

North-star interaction contract

Every room should answer: what do I touch first, what does it produce, where does it go, and how do I leave? The shared shell can own leaving; each room should own meaning.

05Next-step changes

The last hour should buy leverage, not another decorative layer.

P0Shared room overlay contract

Centralize Escape and backdrop dismissal while features retain copy and state. This removes the current 13-way lifecycle drift and gives future modal work one home.

P0One maintained interaction runner

Replace ad hoc scratch probes with a small route matrix that explicitly dismisses tours, tests empty state, primary action, confirmation, reload, and exit.

P1Artifact vocabulary alignment

Make Tarot, I Ching, Games, Dreams, and Garden announce their destination with the same keep language and mirror semantics.

P1Keyboard equivalents for material rooms

Give canvas games and Toybox a minimal keyboard action path without replacing pointer play. Accessibility should be a second instrument, not a second room.

P2Break up Games

Move each attraction into a focused module and leave camera, stage, save prompt, and affinity in the room coordinator. Current games.js is the main maintainability ceiling.

P2Use the report as the product brief

Each pitch is a cuttable promise. Preserve one action and one consequence per room before adding content or another visual pass.
